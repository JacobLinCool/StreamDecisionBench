StreamDecisionBench (SDB)

Evaluating decisions in force on evolving language streams

Language models increasingly run inside applications that keep applying their latest decision while the world changes. SDB scores the decision in force at every instant: an answer that is correct for the state the model saw can arrive late and stay in force after the right decision has changed. Untimed accuracy counts it as correct; the application does not.
